# Node 4.1 — Student Profiles

## In

- **BL-098:** Write the student's feedback protocol into the profile — tested delivery, not just description.

## Out

- **Trait descriptions without operating instructions** — "perfectionist" or "sensitive to feedback" describes the student but leaves every adult to rediscover delivery by trial and error; descriptive-only profile content was excluded as a pattern.
- **Protocols inferred from personality rather than tested in real corrections** — only delivery sequences discovered through actual correction attempts counted as evidence.

## Ambiguous

- Only one fully documented protocol case exists in the corpus (multiple artifacts, narrow student coverage), so the node carries Low confidence and is explicitly incomplete. A second student's tested protocol is the named missing evidence.

# Node 4.2 — Observation & Documentation

## In

- **BL-089:** Verify off-task-looking behavior against the student's baseline before correcting.
- **BL-096:** Put struggling on a clock — fixed-cadence data review, numeric flags, and a timed intervention cycle.
- **BL-101:** Ask the level-fit question directly in session 1 — treat every placement as provisional.
- **BL-116:** Score every promise from both sides — divergence between Guide's and student's POV is the diagnostic trigger.

## Out

- **Correcting surface behavior at face value** — retained only as the anti-pattern; the corpus documents a shutdown produced by an immediate refocus command.
- **Attention-based detection ("noticing when you notice")** — retained only as the anti-pattern, with the late-detection cost recorded in the guides' own retrospectives.
- **Averaging the two POVs, or trusting either by default** — retained only as the anti-pattern (BL-116); the corpus records a student-side "No" standing for a full year, across a guide transition, without investigation.

## Ambiguous

- BL-096 borders on Workshop Assessment's mass-failure trigger (BL-090). Resolved with boundary statements in both: BL-096 fires on one student's drifting daily data; BL-090 fires when many students fail one assessment.
- The node now holds three detection disciplines split by trigger type: BL-089 (in-the-moment behavior vs. documented baseline), BL-096 (numeric drift on a fixed cadence), BL-116 (perception divergence between the Guide's and the student's scores). BL-116's written root-cause closure also feeds BL-115's transition record, so a flagged divergence cannot outlive a guide change unexamined.

# Node 4.3 — Understanding Motivation

## In

- **BL-070:** Decode the mechanism behind the interest, then rebuild it in the work.
- **BL-086:** Identify the student's motivational driver before selecting a lever.

## Out

- **Interest as content (theming) and interest as prize** — excluded as evidence of understanding; BL-070's corpus documents a surface-interest reward failing because the real mechanism was elsewhere.
- **Defending an assumed motivator after the student signals it feels wrong** — retained only as the anti-pattern (BL-086).

## Ambiguous

- The two patterns are near-duplicates discovered independently in two corpora (Student 360 Reviews vs Limitless Meetings). Both kept, with Related notes: BL-086 covers driver identification in conversation; BL-070 is preferred once a specific interest is named, because it decodes and transfers the mechanism.
