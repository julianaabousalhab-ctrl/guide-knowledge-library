# Every Guide Runs a Self-Built Weekly Operating System — Named Responsibilities In, Memory and Reminders Out

## Pattern

The most repeated non-student coaching point in this corpus — three lead guides, five guides — is the same move: stop running recurring responsibilities on memory, and build a personal weekly operating system. The converged design features:

1. **The guide drafts it themselves** — the lead shares the list of required items, the guide builds their own version, the lead reviews and fills gaps. Ownership of the system is part of the system.
2. **Named recurring responsibilities, not vibes** — XP checks, data capture, motivational-model updates, 3C's, parent touchpoints, planning, deadlines — enumerated, scheduled, executed weekly.
3. **The stated purpose is stress and energy, not just compliance** — leads explicitly frame the system as burnout prevention and stress reduction, not surveillance.
4. **Learn the system from stronger operators** — a scheduled weekly call with a team member elsewhere in the org to adopt better organization, planning, and execution systems.

Related: Placed in Follow-Up Coaching beside BL-043, which already extends this node to guide-owned commitments (tracking them to closure). The two are complements at different scales: BL-043 closes *individual* commitments once made; BL-125 installs the *standing weekly system* so the recurring class of commitments — 3C's, motivational-model updates, parent touchpoints, data capture, action items — never depends on memory.

## Anti-Pattern

Reactive organization: responsibilities executed when remembered, "things slipping or getting compressed," deliverable stress carried silently instead of systematized. Named verbatim in Evidence 1 and observed as carried stress in Evidence 4.

## Reusable Coaching Move

1. **Share the canonical list** — the lead enumerates every recurring responsibility for the role.
2. **Guide builds, lead reviews** — the guide drafts their own weekly checklist/system; the lead fills gaps rather than issuing a template.
3. **Frame it as energy protection** — the pitch is sustainable rhythm and lower stress, not compliance monitoring.
4. **Borrow from the best** — schedule recurring time with a strong operator elsewhere in the org and adopt their mechanics.
5. **Review it in 1:1s** — the system itself is a standing coaching object ("is it meeting your needs and reducing stress?").

## Confidence: Medium-High — three lead guides coaching five different guides converged on the same prescription within ten weeks, with the mechanism (self-built, lead-reviewed, energy-framed) explicitly stated by two leads. Capped below High because no instance yet documents the system's effect (fewer misses, lower reported stress).
