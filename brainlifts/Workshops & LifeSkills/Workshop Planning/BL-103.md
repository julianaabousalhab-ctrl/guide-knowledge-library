# Baseline the Life Skill in Week One — Progress Judgments Need a Recorded Start

## Pattern

Before coaching the workshop's life skill, the Guide measures where each student starts on it — in week one, in skill-referenced terms — and records it. Every later checkpoint judgment (Ahead / On target / Behind) is then a comparison against a documented start, not an impression.

The executed design has three parts:

1. **Backwards-map from the AlphaTest:** lock the AlphaTest, then define what demonstrable progress looks like at Week 3 and Week 5.
2. **Measure the baseline in Week 1:** a concrete, observable reading of the skill per student ("made it to the top with guidance," "1/3 of the way up the wall"), captured before instruction has moved anyone.
3. **Judge checkpoints against the recorded start:** Behind/On-target/Ahead mean "relative to where this student began and where the AlphaTest requires them to end" — which is what makes the statuses individually fair and coachable.

The mechanism: without a recorded start, a checkpoint status is a guess that can't distinguish a struggling student from a late starter, and progress on students who began far behind becomes invisible. The tracker's own logic enforces this — where the baseline is missing, honest sheets decline to judge.

Related: BL-088 covers the rehearsal/checkpoint cadence toward AlphaTest conditions; this pattern supplies its measurement anchor. BL-071 covers the student-facing pass criteria; the baseline is the Guide-facing start point. The 1:1 parallel is BL-029 — a credible goal is "grounded in current performance"; this is the same rule at workshop scale.

## Evidence

*1- The design is mandated by the instrument, in exactly this order*

The tracker instructions: "1. Lock in your workshop's AlphaTest … 2. Backwards map skill checkpoints… Week 1: Baseline = where are students starting on their life skill?… 4. Use the data to address individual student needs, alter workshop pace, etc."

Source artifact: Workshop Skill Trackers SY25-26 — Instructions sheet

*2- Executed at scale, in skill-referenced terms*

2,340 recorded week-one baselines across 378 workshop sheets (~20 campuses, S3–S5), written as observable skill readings — e.g. Climb Quest baselines distinguishing "made it to the top with guidance," "touched top but did not [rappel]," and "1/3 of the way up" — fine-grained enough for later checkpoints to mean something per student.

Source artifact: Workshop Skill Trackers SY25-26 — Week 1: Baseline columns

*3- The system declines to judge without a start*

Where a student missed the baseline week, honest sheets mark the next checkpoint "Not enough data" rather than assigning a status — the tracker treating the baseline as a precondition for judgment, not a formality.

Source artifact:[Workshop Skill Tracker](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1S-FiD2L3ziI8c8q97QwKJqFayLBf2_gaOQ0rf2KehGI/edit?usp=sharing)

## Anti-Pattern

The anti-pattern is judging without a start: checkpoint statuses assigned to students who have no recorded baseline.

*1- Measured in this corpus*

106 students carry Ahead / On target / Behind checkpoint statuses with an empty baseline cell — a progress judgment with no documented reference point, indistinguishable from impression.

Source artifact: Workshop Skill Trackers SY25-26 — baseline vs checkpoint columns

*2- The cost this produces downstream*

A status without a start cannot be defended to the student or continued by another Guide — it reproduces at workshop scale the failure BL-032 documents in 1:1 coaching: diagnosing from impressions rather than observed evidence.

Source artifact: cross-reference BL-032 (observe before diagnosing); within-corpus, the 106 unanchored statuses

## Reusable Coaching Move

Run the Baseline Gate in week one of every workshop:

1. **Before teaching:** put every student through one observable attempt at (a component of) the AlphaTest skill.
2. **Record it in skill terms** — what the student can do, not a rating ("reads 3 of 5 holds," not "weak").
3. **Refuse unanchored statuses:** if a student has no baseline (absence, late join), the next entry is "Not enough data — baseline on [date]," never a guessed status.
4. **At each checkpoint, judge against the start:** "given where they began, is this student's trajectory reaching the AlphaTest?"

## Confidence: Medium — mandated by the instrument and executed 2,340 times across ~20 campuses, with a small measured anti-pattern set (106); the corpus does not compare outcomes of baselined vs unbaselined tracking, so the value claim rests on the mechanism and the system's own "Not enough data" logic.
