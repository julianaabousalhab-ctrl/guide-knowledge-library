# Node 7.1 — Behavioral Planning

## In

- **BL-073:** Write the behavior plan in observable "looks like" terms with a measurable passing bar.

## Out

- **The consequence system as the plan** — issuing cards repeatedly with no recorded target, replacement behavior, or passing bar was retained only as the anti-pattern; where it occurs in the corpus, the documented behavior does not change.
- **Perfection-demanding criteria** — excluded; the included plans build in tolerance (e.g. 12 of 15 days) rather than requiring unbroken streaks.

## Ambiguous

- Plans come from campuses with different behavioral systems (cards, strikes, graduated goals). Included when the architecture matched (observable definition + replacement behavior + numeric bar), regardless of the local system's vocabulary.

# Node 7.2 — Incident Management

## In

- **BL-075:** Separate the students and take each account individually, then corroborate with uninvolved witnesses.
- **BL-076:** Make the first parent message complete — facts, actions taken, and what happens next.
- **BL-078:** After an incident, change the conditions — not only the student.

## Out

- **Accepting the first (often minimizing) account** — retained only as the anti-pattern; the corpus documents intent surfacing only on later, deeper questioning.
- **Accurate-but-incomplete first messages** — excluded as acceptable practice; the corpus records the trust cost when parents learn a material fact later.
- **Warnings-and-conversations-only responses to known risky behavior** — retained only as the anti-pattern, ending in a documented injury.

## Ambiguous

- Message completeness depends on internal information flow (a fact staff held never reached the sender). Treated as part of the pattern's completeness bar rather than as an excusing condition.

# Node 7.3 — Behavioral Intervention

## In

- **BL-077:** Walk the student to the posted standard and let them classify their own behavior.
- **BL-095:** Calibrate consequences across Guides — same behavior, same response.

## Out

- **Verdict-delivery to an already-escalated student** — retained only as the anti-pattern; the corpus documents the announcement itself triggering the next card.
- **Per-Guide thresholds and case-by-case negotiation** — excluded; students track the drift precisely, and the corpus records it corroding behavior and culture.

## Ambiguous

- BL-077 depends on BL-095: self-classification only works if the standard means the same thing at every Guide's hands. The dependency is stated in BL-095 rather than merging the two, because the Guide-facing moves differ (a conversation structure vs a team calibration practice).

# Node 7.4 — Emotional Regulation

## In

- **BL-074:** Move regulation upstream — teach signal recognition and pre-select the tools before the moment.
- **BL-087:** Turn an emotional trigger into a reset-and-return routine.

## Out

- **In-the-moment directives to a dysregulated student** — retained only as the anti-pattern; the corpus documents refusals of "take a break" / "use your strategies" delivered mid-escalation.
- **Compliance-first responses to distress** — excluded (recorded as BL-087's anti-pattern).
- **Eliminating the emotion as the goal** — out of scope by both patterns' own statements; the target is preventing the emotion from determining what happens next.

## Ambiguous

- Two corpora produced the same mechanism (behavior plans vs 1:1 meetings). Both kept with Related notes: BL-074 is the plan-level installation and the primary pattern; BL-087 is the conversational route to co-creating the routine.
