# Node 6.3.4 — Culture Building Ritual

## In

- **BL-097:** Ritualize mistake-sharing — give failure a recurring, public, celebrated slot.

## Out

- **Verbal reassurance ("mistakes are okay") as a culture practice** — retained only as the anti-pattern; end-of-session surveys at four campuses record students who heard the message all year and still answered that mistakes aren't okay.
- **Celebrating the error alone** — excluded; the included designs celebrate the recovery arc (what went wrong → what changed → what happened next), not failure for its own sake.
- **Ad hoc mistake conversations** — excluded as a pattern; the converged designs are structural (a named, recurring, calendared slot), not occasional.

## Ambiguous

- The ritual could plausibly sit under Reflection or Growth Mindset. Placed here because the defining feature in the evidence is structural placement in the community rhythm (a slot like the shoutout ritual), not the reflective content.
- Individual variants (one student's "failure made collectible" plan) sit at the boundary with Behavioral Planning; included as supporting evidence because the mechanism — public, celebrated normalization — is the same.
